## Description
Transit Times answers one question fast: how long to get there from where I am?

Pick five destinations and choose train or car for each. The app and its widgets show:

• Travel time from your current location, updated as you move
• Subway: next train times, colored line badges and live MTA service alerts (New York)
• Car: live traffic conditions, light, moderate or heavy, with the minutes it adds
• Leave now, Leave at, or Arrive by: plan a trip for later and see when to head out
• Home Screen widgets (medium, large, single destination) and Lock Screen widgets
• One tap to open turn-by-turn directions in Google Maps

Powered by Google Maps Platform. You use your own Google API key, so there are no accounts, ads or tracking, and your locations stay on your device.

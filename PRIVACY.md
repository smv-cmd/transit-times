# Privacy Policy — Transit Times

_Last updated: October 7, 2026_

Transit Times shows travel times from your current location to destinations you choose. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

## What the app uses

- **Your location.** Used only to calculate travel times from where you are. It is kept on your device (including a shared container so the widgets can use it) and is never sent to the developer.
- **Your destinations and Google API key.** You enter these in the app. They are stored on your device only.

## Who receives data

- **Google Maps Platform (Routes API).** To calculate a route, the app sends your current coordinates, the destination address, the travel mode and the departure or arrival time directly from your device to Google, using the API key you provide. Google's handling of that data is described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **MTA (New York).** For subway service alerts the app downloads the MTA's public alerts feed. No personal data is sent.

The developer operates no servers and does not receive, collect or store any of your data.

## Your choices

- Turn off location access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services. Travel times will then fall back to the last saved location or stop updating.
- Delete the app to remove all data it stored on your device.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
